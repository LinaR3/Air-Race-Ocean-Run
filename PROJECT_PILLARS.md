# Production pillars

These five pillars are the quality bar and order of work for Air Race Ocean Run.

1. **Visual direction** — Design a polished, coherent 3D low-poly air-race world over open water. Camera and scene composition must keep the next checkpoint readable while making the ocean feel expansive.
2. **Asset selection** — Use reviewed assets with known sources and licenses. Keep the pylon, float, water, sky, and aircraft styles coherent; do not substitute weak placeholders for approved art.
3. **Presentation scene** — Build the ocean course and a composed presentation view first. Review the map without the aircraft before integrating aircraft gameplay.
4. **Gameplay** — Level 1 is a closed course with six checkpoint pairs, one finish marker, a 60-second limit, energy management, cans, and optional barrel-roll combos.
5. **Polish** — Refine route readability, smooth aircraft response, VFX, HUD, retry flow, and performance. Wwise is the final production step.

## Work sequence

Asset registry and project setup → map and presentation scene → aircraft integration → gameplay and UI → visual/audio polish → Wwise.

## Boundaries

- This is a fictional portfolio simulation, not an official Red Bull campaign.
- Do not edit or copy scenes from the earlier car project.
- No 2D parallax for this project; depth comes from the 3D ocean, skybox, camera, route, and distant set dressing.
- The gameplay map is built before the aircraft is integrated.
- Wwise is deferred until the final phase.
