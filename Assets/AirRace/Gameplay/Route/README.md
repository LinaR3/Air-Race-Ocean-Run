# Level 01 route

The JSON file records the current Level 01 layout. The Unity scene places six checkpoint gates around a broad oval. Adjacent gate centers are 200–206 m apart (6.25–6.44 seconds at 32 m/s cruise), not sub-second hops. The six segment lengths are 206, 200, 206, 206, 200, and 206 m. Each pair has a 24 m opening. The seventh marker is a gold finish gate near the start.

The run lasts 60 seconds. Correct checkpoint passes award 10 points and restore 26 flight-energy points. Flight energy drains at 3.5 points per second and pauses during the active 3-second boost. Collecting an energy can awards 25 points, 30 energy, and one stored boost charge (maximum three). Each completed axial roll adds 10 points times the current fast-chain multiplier and gives a small upward lift. Repeated Space presses inside the 0.9-second combo window chain rolls; Space does not activate without a can charge.

The aircraft now uses its pitch and bank to determine its actual travel direction: W/S pitch the nose, A/D bank into a coordinated turn, Left Shift brakes and tightens a held turn, and Space spends a collected charge for the boost/barrel-roll stunt. The rear chase camera keeps the horizon level, while a compact route radar shows the ordered gates and remaining can pickups. Can pickups sit near the flight corridor between gates. The 4.8 km square ocean extends beyond an invisible square flight boundary 900 m from the origin. The separate `GAME_RULES_LEVEL01.md` remains the design source for future tuning.
