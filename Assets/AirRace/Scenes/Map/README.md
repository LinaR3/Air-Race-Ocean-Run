# Level 01 map scene

`AirRace_Level01_Map.unity` is the first playable course scene. It contains a 4.8 km square of A.U.R.W. ocean, the selected stylized sky, six ordered gates built from the reusable pylon + swim-raft prefab, a separate gold finish gate, six energy-can pickups near the flight corridor, the player aircraft, and a low rear chase camera that keeps the horizon level. The aircraft scale review remains a separate presentation scene.

The route is a broad closed oval. Its checkpoint centers are 200–206 m apart. The aircraft starts 120 m before Checkpoint 01, leaving time to line up with the first gate. The ocean surface spans 4.8 km by 4.8 km. Flight turns inward at an invisible ±900 m boundary, and the water edge sits outside both the camera far clip and fog horizon.

The player aircraft prefab comes from the approved free FBX sample, linked directly to its imported source mesh. This avoids the broken generated-mesh reference that previously made the aircraft invisible. The livery uses the saved metallic blue material, red accents, and yellow details. The propeller is visible but does not rotate yet; its safe separation and animation remain a follow-up. The generic energy can is original low-poly blockout art with a blue body, red band, yellow top, and emissive amber ring. It is intentionally unbranded.

The game opens on a control card. Press Enter to start a three-second countdown; the flight timer and energy drain begin after it. Controls: W/S pitch the aircraft to climb or dive, A/D bank into a turn, Left Shift brakes and makes a held turn tighter, and Space activates a stored can boost and barrel roll. The flight HUD shows speed, altitude, time, score and energy; its lower-left route radar shows the ordered checkpoints and visible can pickups. The Figma UI is deferred to the separately agreed next stage.
